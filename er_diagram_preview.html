📦 The 14 Principal Entities Quick Reference
1. Conferences
PK: conference_id
Root event metadata, acronym, year, venue, deadlines, start & end dates.
2. Tracks
PK: track_id
FK: conference_id
Thematic disciplines (e.g. AI/ML, Networks, Security). 1:N with Conference.
3. Topics
PK: topic_id
FK: track_id
Granular research keywords used for reviewer expertise matching.
4. Authors
PK: author_id
FK: user_id (1:1 with Users)
Researcher profile, ORCID ID, bio, and affiliation.
5. Papers
PK: paper_id
FK: track_id, primary_author_id
Master submission record tracking paper title, abstract, and lifecycle status.
6. Versions (Paper_Versions)
PK: version_id
FK: paper_id
Tracks PDF revisions (Initial draft, revision, camera-ready) with changelogs.
7. Reviewers
PK: reviewer_id
FK: user_id (1:1 with Users)
Peer evaluator profile maintaining max workload limit (default: 5).
8. Expertise (Reviewer_Expertise)
PK: (reviewer_id, topic_id)
FKs: reviewer_id, topic_id
M:N competence mapping: EXPERT, KNOWLEDGEABLE, INFORMED.
9. Conflicts (Reviewer_Conflicts)
PK: conflict_id
FKs: reviewer_id, author_id
Bars reviewers from evaluating co-authors or same-institution authors.
10. Assignments (Paper_Assignments)
PK: assignment_id
FKs: paper_id, reviewer_id
Dispatches eligible reviewer to paper; enforces workload & COI barrier.
11. Reviews
PK: review_id
FK: assignment_id (1:1 UNIQUE)
Evaluation scores (1-10) and separated author vs chair comments.
12. Decisions
PK: decision_id
FK: paper_id (1:1 UNIQUE), chair_id
Official verdict: ACCEPT, REJECT, REVISE with chair rationale.
13. Registrations
PK: registration_id
FKs: conference_id, user_id, paper_id
Tracks delegate conference fees, paper clearance, and attendance.
14. Presentation_Slots
PK: slot_id
FKs: session_id, paper_id (UNIQUE)
Hall, session, and time interval allocated to accepted papers.
Supporting: Users
PK: user_id
Central authentication & demographics uniting Authors, Reviewers, Chairs.
📋 Relational Schema & Key Constraints
Table Name Primary Key Foreign Keys & Cardinality Key Domain / Business Constraints Normal Form
Conferences conference_id None UNIQUE(acronym, year), CHECK(end_date >= start_date) 3NF
Tracks track_id conference_id (1:N) UNIQUE(conference_id, track_name) 3NF
Topics topic_id track_id (1:N) UNIQUE(track_id, topic_name) 3NF
Users user_id None email UNIQUE, role IN ('CHAIR','AUTHOR','REVIEWER',...) 3NF
Papers paper_id track_id, primary_author_id status IN ('SUBMITTED','UNDER_REVIEW','ACCEPTED','REJECTED') 3NF
Paper_Authors (paper_id, author_id) paper_id, author_id (M:N) UNIQUE(paper_id, author_order), author_order >= 1 3NF
Paper_Versions version_id paper_id (1:N) UNIQUE(paper_id, version_num), version_num >= 1 3NF
Reviewers reviewer_id user_id (1:1 UNIQUE) max_workload > 0 (DEFAULT 5) 3NF
Reviewer_Expertise (reviewer_id, topic_id) reviewer_id, topic_id (M:N) expertise_level IN ('EXPERT','KNOWLEDGEABLE','INFORMED') 3NF
Reviewer_Conflicts conflict_id reviewer_id, author_id UNIQUE(reviewer_id, author_id) 3NF
Paper_Assignments assignment_id paper_id, reviewer_id UNIQUE(paper_id, reviewer_id), status IN ('ASSIGNED','COMPLETED',...) 3NF
Reviews review_id assignment_id (1:1 UNIQUE) CHECK originality, soundness, clarity, overall BETWEEN 1 AND 10 3NF
Decisions decision_id paper_id (1:1 UNIQUE), chair_id decision_type IN ('ACCEPT','REJECT','MAJOR_REVISION','MINOR_REVISION') 3NF
Presentation_Slots slot_id session_id, paper_id (1:1 UNIQUE) CHECK end_time > start_time, UNIQUE(session_id, presentation_order) 3NF
